About me

Hi, I'm Nathaniel! I'm a first-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo, interested in robotics across mechanical design, electronics, and software.

I got into robotics through VEX in high school, where I became president and programming lead of the Unionville High School team. I managed 40 members, led a team of 8 programmers, and we competed at the provincial level three years in a row. I also joined FIRST Robotics Competition Team Minekee, where I worked on Java robot code, camera-based localization, and wiring up the intake.

I like building things quickly. In one week I designed and 3D printed a pair of articulating wings for FanExpo Canada. I've also made a glove that controls a mouse by rotating, and a game that placed second at a 48-hour game jam. You can see them on the projects page.

I've also spent my summers as a camp counselor with the City of Richmond Hill, working with kids ages 4 to 12.
